S2.1 physical card boundary reference

R&D dataset review only. Review the physical material edge, not the printed frame. Detector candidates and provisional fits are not human references. Blur/shadow ambiguity may be indeterminate. This editor is never a required production processing step.

Review all four edges and corners, adjust each line, choose its certainty, then explicitly confirm each side. Export creates a new annotation version. All coordinates remain in original-image pixels; display zoom does not change the original.

Original scan and independent lines

Place two points to create a line. Drag either handle independently or drag the line to translate it. Empty-space drag pans. Wheel zooms at the pointer. Arrow keys nudge the active line by 1 original pixel, or 0.1 with Shift. Thick outlined REF lines: green confirmed, yellow unconfirmed, orange uncertain. Thin colored patterned lines: detector candidates, never human confirmations.

F/E prefixes identify frozen/experimental reports; the number identifies the input report. IDs such as F1:L0 match the menu and line labels. Colors repeat across reports; IDs are report-local and do not imply correspondence. The main view shows candidates for the active side, edge panes show their own side, and corner panes show the outgoing side. Labels occupy a separate gutter so they do not cover the scan. Overlapping fits can share pixels; selection draws the chosen candidate last and dims the rest. Visibility controls never alter reference geometry.
