# SELBY V0.5b — operator label printing

1. Sign in with an authorized backend operator account. For imported local orders, use **Labels → select ORD → Open label previews / printing**, or **Orders → select the confirmed mapped order → Print labels / preview…**. The Labels screen displays the SLB-C → PC pairs, current backend blockers and read-only previews. Open the intended SP in **Incoming Orders**, then **Print labels…**. This does not change reconciliation, local production identities or grading records. Reconciliation must already be complete. Review backend blockers; payment and inspection eligibility cannot be overridden here.
2. Explicitly select **Munbyn ITPP941**. In Windows, use 50 × 30 mm media and calibrate the loaded roll according to the printer instructions. The workstation saves this exact device name in its desktop INI. Missing devices require explicit selection; there is no fallback.
3. Select one order/card row and choose **Preview selected label**. Check the full SP/PC reference and QR payload shown under the preview. Repeat for each required card; identical copies must show different PC references. Voided identities must not appear. The order label's count includes active physical cards only.
4. For one order label or selected cards, select the intended row(s) and choose **Print selected labels…**. **Print all eligible card labels…** excludes the order label. Review the confirmation's complete references, printer, 50 × 30 mm dimensions, number of labels and any reprint reason. Confirm explicitly. Do not approve a batch as the first physical calibration test.
5. A `submitted` result means the software handed the job to the printer, not that paper came out. Measure the first test label, inspect clipping/text and scan/decode the QR. Compare the complete decoded identity with the original preview. Attach it to the order bag, card sleeve or protective packaging only; never directly to a trading card.
6. After physically inspecting and decoding **every label in that exact job**, select its submitted job in the history selector and choose **Mark selected submitted job physically verified…**. Confirm only if all identities match. The backend records the authenticated operator assertion.
7. Reprints require a concrete reason, such as a damaged sleeve or unreadable output after queue inspection. Select the exact identities, enter the reason and confirm again. Earlier claimed jobs require reasons even if their output is uncertain or failed. Do not remove the history or local journal to bypass the reason.
8. On any network/printer error, wait, inspect the Windows queue and physical output, and refresh backend history. Do not repeatedly click Print or restart to attempt automatic recovery. A claim response lost before submission does not grant permission to resubmit the same job. An uncertain native submission can have produced some labels. Review `label-print-journal.jsonl` beside `desktop.ini` with support if backend and local outcomes differ; keep it intact. After review, any new attempt is an explicitly confirmed reprint with its own reason.
